A newspaper front page — "WORLD TODAY" — summing up the world news of Wednesday, 18 November 2026 in a single 1024×1024 image. The lead story covers Trump landing in Shenzhen to a welcome from Xi Jinping, with Putin joining them as APEC opens ahead of the first US–China–Russia trilateral in history, illustrated by a navy Shenzhen skyline band with three overlapping circles for the three leaders. Six side stories cover drones over the Gulf and Ukraine, the Gaza truce, Democrats taking the Senate, Cyclone Akvan lashing Odisha, and NVIDIA after Huang, with a footer ticker on the Quad in Manila, US measles status, and the Siberia crash. The image is generated deterministically by `artwork.py` using Pillow.
